# People

Documents written by individuals about their own work, growth, and trajectory. These are the personal reflection artifacts of a company — the ones that surface in performance cycles, career conversations, and self-directed development. They exist at the intersection of accountability and growth: honest enough to be useful, structured enough to be actionable.

## Audience

The individual writing the document, their manager, and anyone else involved in a performance or career conversation. These docs are written in first person or close third, with the goal of giving the reader an accurate picture of who this person is, what they accomplished, and where they are headed.

## Document Types

| Document | Purpose | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Self-Assessment (`sa`) | A quarterly or annual reflection on what happened, how the individual showed up, what they learned, and where they are heading. Written as a narrative with structured sections rather than a list of accomplishments. | ✅ `sa_pbj-co.md` |
